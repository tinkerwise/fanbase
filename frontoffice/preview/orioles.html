The Brass

David Rubenstein
Chairman & Managing Partner
Since 2024
Carlyle Group co-founder; led the group that purchased the Orioles from the Angelos family for ~$1.7 billion in 2024.
Mike Elias
Executive VP & General Manager
Since November 2018
Hired from the Houston Astros, where he was Assistant GM. Oversaw the rebuild that produced 101 wins in 2023 and the AL East title.
Sig Mejdal
VP, Analytics & Development
Since November 2018
Analytics pioneer who joined Elias from Houston. Architect of the data-driven scouting and development model.
Matt Blood
VP, Player Development
Since 2019
Oversees the minor league system, which has been one of the deepest in baseball under the Elias era.
Brad Ciolek
Director, Amateur Scouting
Since 2019
Led the draft rooms that selected Adley Rutschman (#1 overall, 2019), Colton Cowser, and Coby Mayo.
Brandon Hyde
Manager
Since October 2018
Third-base coach in Chicago before Elias hired him. Posted 40+ wins in each of his first four seasons; 101-61 in 2023.
